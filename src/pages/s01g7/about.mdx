  <h1>ABOUT US</h1>
  <p class="sub">CSARCH2 — Group 7 [S01] — 3rd Term, A.Y. 2025–2026</p>

  <div class="section-divider"></div>

  <p class="about-text" style="text-align: justify;">
    <strong style="color: var(--text);">Full Capacity: The Evolution of Computer Data Storage</strong> is
    a virtual exhibit created in partial fulfillment of the course requirements for Introduction to
    Computer Organization and Architecture 2 (CSARCH2) at De La Salle University – Manila.
  </p>

  <p class="about-text" style="text-align: justify;">
    The exhibit traces the history of data storage from punch cards and magnetic drums to solid-state
    drives and emerging technologies like DNA storage, highlighting the architectural principles and
    trade-offs behind each innovation.
  </p>

  <div class="members">
    <h2>AI Usage Declaration</h2>
    <p class="about-text" style="text-align: justify;">
      In the development of this exhibit, the group used AI as a supporting tool throughout the design and development process. AI
      assistance was primarily used for debugging layout, rendering, and state-management
      issues; drafting and refining simulator and animation logic; writing explanatory inline code comments;
      improving UI spacing, contrast, and responsive behavior; and reviewing the clarity,
      grammar, and terminology of our written content.
    </p>
    <p class="about-text" style="text-align: justify;">
      All AI-assisted code and content were manually reviewed, tested locally, and verified
      against our cited sources before being committed to the project. AI tools were treated
      as a guide for troubleshooting and refinement, not as a substitute for our own
      understanding, and final implementation decisions, exhibit content, and simulator
      behavior were confirmed and adjusted by each member individually.
    </p>
