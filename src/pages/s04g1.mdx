    <div className="comparison__grid">
      <div className="comparison__card">

          <h3>Ripple Carry Adder</h3>
        </div>
        <p className="card__desc">Each Full Adder waits for the carry-out from the previous stage before computing its sum bit.</p>
        <div className="card__lists">
          <div className="list__group">
            <h4>Advantages</h4>
            <ul>
              <li className="pos">Simple circuit design</li>
              <li className="pos">Fewer logic gates</li>
              <li className="pos">Easy to implement</li>
            </ul>
          </div>
          <div className="list__group trade-offs">
            <h4>Trade-offs</h4>
            <ul>
              <li className="neg">Slow &mdash; each carry must ripple forward</li>
              <li className="neg">Delay grows linearly with bit width</li>
            </ul>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div className="comparison__card">
        <div className="card__header">
          <span className="badge badge--cla">CLA</span>
          <h3>Carry Lookahead Adder</h3>
        </div>
        <p className="card__desc">Uses Generate (G) and Propagate (P) signals to calculate all carry values simultaneously before summing.</p>
        <div className="card__lists">
          <div className="list__group">
            <h4>Advantages</h4>
            <ul>
              <li className="pos">Fast &mdash; all carries computed at once</li>
              <li className="pos">Reduced carry-path depth through parallel logic</li>
              <li className="pos">Used in real CPUs</li>
            </ul>
          </div>
          <div className="list__group trade-offs">
            <h4>Trade-offs</h4>
            <ul>

              <li className="neg">More wiring overhead</li>
            </ul>

        <p className="timeline__body">Electromechanical relays open and close to perform binary addition &mdash; slow, audible, and the first machines to compute with switches instead of gears.</p>
      </div>

      <div className="timeline__card">
        <div className="timeline__header">
          <span className="timeline__year mono-value">1945</span>
          <h3>Vacuum-tube arithmetic</h3>
        </div>
        <p className="timeline__body">ENIAC's accumulators add and subtract using thousands of tubes, trading relay speed limits for heat and constant tube failures.</p>
      </div>

      <div className="timeline__card">
        <div className="timeline__header">
          <span className="timeline__year mono-value">1958-63</span>
          <h3>Discrete transistors</h3>
        </div>
        <p className="timeline__body">Transistorized logic shrinks the arithmetic unit onto circuit boards small enough to fit inside a single cabinet.</p>
      </div>

      <div className="timeline__card">
        <div className="timeline__header">
          <span className="timeline__year mono-value">1970</span>
          <h3>The 74181 IC</h3>
        </div>
        <p className="timeline__body">A single 24-pin chip packages a full 4-bit ALU &mdash; sixteen arithmetic and logic operations selectable by four control lines.</p>
      </div>

      <div className="timeline__card">
        <div className="timeline__header">
          <span className="timeline__year mono-value">1971-today</span>
          <h3>Embedded in the CPU</h3>
        </div>
        <p className="timeline__body">From the Intel 4004 onward, the ALU stops being a separate unit and becomes a block on the processor's own die.</p>
